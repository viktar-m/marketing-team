# Brand brief: Acme Pantry (fictional sample)

> **This is a fictional company** used to demonstrate and test the marketing team. Replace it with your own brief at `marketing/brand-brief.md`.

## 1. Company and product
- **What we sell:** Acme Pantry is a meal-planning app that builds a weekly plan from what's already in your kitchen, then writes the shopping list for the gaps.
- **Category:** meal-planning app
- **Key products / plans:** Free (1 plan per week, 20 saved recipes); Plus at $6/month (unlimited plans, household sharing, nutrition targets)
- **Core features:** pantry tracking by barcode or photo; weekly plan generator; auto shopping list sorted by store aisle; household sharing; leftover suggestions; dietary filters (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal)
- **What we don't do:** no grocery delivery; no medical or clinical nutrition advice; no calorie counting beyond basic nutrition targets
- **Website:** https://acmepantry.example
- **Key pages:** /pricing, /features, /blog, /help

## 2. Audience
**Busy parents**
- **Who:** working parents of 2+ kids planning family dinners
- **Trying to:** get dinner on the table without a daily "what's for dinner" scramble
- **Pains:** food going off in the fridge, repeated shopping trips, picky eaters
- **Objections:** "Another app I'll stop using after a week"
- **Hang out:** Facebook parenting groups, Instagram, school WhatsApp groups

**Budget-conscious young professionals**
- **Who:** 22–35, first apartment, cooking for one or two
- **Trying to:** spend less on takeaway and waste less food
- **Pains:** buying ingredients for one recipe and binning the rest
- **Objections:** "Meal planning takes longer than just ordering"
- **Hang out:** LinkedIn, X, Reddit personal-finance communities

## 3. Positioning
- **One-line positioning:** For busy households who waste food and time on dinner decisions, Acme Pantry is the meal-planning app that starts from what you already have, unlike recipe apps that start from a blank shopping list.
- **Top 3 differentiators:**
  1. Plans start from your pantry — proof: pantry-first generator (core feature)
  2. Shopping list sorted by aisle — proof: aisle sorting for major chains
  3. Built for households — proof: shared lists and plans on the Plus plan
- **Alternatives:** generic recipe apps (great recipes, no pantry awareness); spreadsheets and notes apps (flexible, but manual); meal-kit subscriptions (convenient, but pricier and more packaging)

## 4. Voice and style
- **Voice in three words:** warm, practical, unfussy
- **We sound like:** "Got half a bag of spinach and two eggs? That's a frittata. We'll plan the other six dinners around what's left."
- **We never sound like:** "Revolutionize your culinary journey with our AI-powered, best-in-class solution!"
- **Do:** short sentences, real kitchen examples, second person ("you")
- **Don't:** guilt about food waste, diet-culture language, hype words (revolutionary, game-changing, best-in-class), exclamation marks in headlines
- **Terminology:** "Acme Pantry" (never "AcmePantry" or "the Acme app"); "plan", not "menu"; "shopping list", not "grocery manifest"
- **Headings:** sentence case
- **Spelling:** US English

## 5. SEO
