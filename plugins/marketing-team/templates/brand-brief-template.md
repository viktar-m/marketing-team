# Brand brief: <Company or product name>

> The single source of truth every agent reads before working. Keep it factual and current. Update it when the product, audience or voice changes. Sections marked (required) are needed for good output; the rest make it better.

## 1. Company and product (required)
- **What we sell:** one-sentence description
- **Category:** how a buyer would describe the category
- **Key products / plans:** names, one line each
- **Core features:** the 5–10 features that matter most, in plain words
- **What we don't do:** limits worth stating so agents don't overclaim
- **Website:** main URL
- **Key pages:** pricing, product, docs, blog, contact (URLs)

## 2. Audience (required)
For each segment:
- **Who:** role, company type, experience level
- **What they're trying to get done:**
- **Pains:** what frustrates them today
- **Objections:** why they hesitate to buy
- **Where they hang out:** channels, communities, publications

## 3. Positioning (required)
- **One-line positioning:** for [audience] who [need], [product] is the [category] that [key benefit], unlike [alternative]
- **Top 3 differentiators:** each with the proof behind it
- **Main alternatives and competitors:** name + how we compare, honestly

## 4. Voice and style (required)
- **Voice in three words:**
- **We sound like:** short example sentence or paragraph
- **We never sound like:** short counter-example
- **Do:** (e.g., active voice, concrete examples, short sentences)
- **Don't:** (e.g., hype words, exclamation marks, jargon without explanation)
- **Terminology:** preferred terms, product name spelling and capitalisation, words to avoid
- **Headings:** sentence case or title case
- **Spelling:** US or UK English

## 5. SEO
- **Priority topics:** themes we want to own
- **Target keywords:** primary keywords per topic
- **Competitor domains to benchmark:**
- **Pages that matter most commercially:**

## 6. Social media
- **Active platforms:** LinkedIn / X / Facebook (handles or page URLs)
- **Goal per platform:** awareness, traffic, leads, community
- **Posting cadence:** e.g., LinkedIn 3×/week, X daily, Facebook 2×/week
- **Hashtags we use:**
- **Content pillars:** 3–5 recurring themes (education, product, customer stories, behind the scenes…)

## 7. Proof and assets
- **Approved stats and claims:** with sources
- **Customer stories we may name:**
- **Visual identity:** summary only — full detail lives in `marketing/design/brand-kit.md` (logo files, colours, fonts, image style)

## 8. Calls to action
- **Primary CTA:** e.g., Start free trial — URL
- **Secondary CTAs:** e.g., Book a demo, Download guide

## 9. Paid and sales
- **Ad platforms in use:** Google Ads / LinkedIn Ads / Meta Ads
- **Typical budget range and goals:** e.g., leads at under $X CPA
- **Sales motion:** self-serve, sales-led, partner; who the sales team sells to
- **Release rhythm:** how often the product ships notable updates

## 10. AI visibility
- **Brand names and aliases** as people write them (including common misspellings)
- **Competitors to benchmark** (names + domains)
- **Questions our customers ask AI assistants** — 10–30 real questions, in their words

## 11. Rules and red lines
- Legal or compliance requirements, claims we may not make, topics to avoid
