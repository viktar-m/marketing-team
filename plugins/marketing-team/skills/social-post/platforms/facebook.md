# Facebook

Platform specs change. Treat the numbers below as working defaults and check the platform's current guidance when precision matters.

## Audience and tone
Broader and more personal than LinkedIn; people scroll among friends' and family posts. Warm, plain-spoken, visual. Community and conversation outperform announcements.

## Length and layout
- Very long posts are allowed, but only the first lines show before "See more" (roughly 100–150 characters on mobile).
- Sweet spot: 40–250 characters for engagement posts; longer is fine for stories and helpful how-tos.
- Short paragraphs with blank lines between them. Emojis are more accepted here than on LinkedIn, if the brand voice allows.

## Formats
- **Image post:** the default. Square (1080×1080) or portrait (1080×1350). Real, warm photos beat stock.
- **Short video / Reel:** vertical 9:16, under 60 seconds, captions on, hook in the first 2–3 seconds.
- **Link post:** a short intro plus the link preview — check the preview image and title look right.
- **Question or poll:** prompts comments, which help reach.
- **Album / carousel:** 3–10 images for step-by-steps, before/after or collections.

## Links
Link posts are fine here, especially for blog traffic. Keep the intro text short and the value of clicking clear.

## Hashtags
0–3, if any. Hashtags matter less on Facebook; a brand hashtag is enough.

## CTA patterns
Ask a warm, easy question ("What's your go-to weeknight dinner?"), invite people to tag someone who'd find it useful, or point to the link.
